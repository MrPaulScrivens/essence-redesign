4Where You Actually Are

There is a reason the confusion has felt so personal. You think the stopping and restarting, the not having it figured out yet, means something is wrong with you specifically.

It doesn’t. You are on a path that has stages, and almost nobody is ever shown the path.

This is the Soul-Led Entrepreneur Journey. Six stages. Read it slowly and notice where you feel yourself standing.

Stage One. The Calling.

It arrives like a knowing you didn’t choose. An idea, an offering, placed in you by God, that simply will not leave you alone. This is where you finally say yes to it. Sometimes the first time you have ever chosen yourself.

Stage Two. The Clarity. you might be here

This stage humbles you. Clarity does not arrive in your head while you sit and think about it. It arrives through action, through the audacity to try, one session and one post at a time. You gather breadcrumbs that slowly reveal who you are for and what you are really here to do.

Stage Three. The Foundation. or you might be here

You have found the thread. Now you build. The brand, the voice, the offer, the real bones of a business, all while your visibility anxiety screams in the background. This is exactly where the nervous system work stops being optional, because the block was never in the strategy. It lives in the body.

Stage Four. The Authority.

You show up so consistently that something shifts in you that no strategy could have created. Your gifts stop feeling like questions and start feeling like certainties. The money hasn’t caught up to the effort yet, and this is the messy middle that lives loudest right here.

Stage Five. The Flow.

The compound interest of every stage before it finally pays out. People seek you out. They arrive already ready, because you built something real and you kept showing up for it long enough to be found.

Stage Six. The Expansion.

Everything you survived and everything you became turns into something bigger than you. The business becomes a vehicle for more than money. It becomes legacy. One woman rising and reaching back for the next.

Sit with where you landed.

If you saw yourself in Stage Two or Stage Three, you are exactly who this guide was written for. The Clarity and the Foundation are the two stages where almost everyone gets stuck. And they are the two stages where the body work becomes the whole thing, because this is where you are being asked to be seen before your nervous system believes it is safe.

Here is the part that matters most.

Nearly half of new businesses don’t make it. Not because those women weren’t built for it. Because nobody gave them the map. Nobody told them the confusion was a stage and not a verdict, or that the inconsistency was never proof they should quit.

You are standing in the exact place the journey gets hard for everyone, holding your breath, wondering if the fact that it’s hard means it isn’t yours.

It’s yours.

Now let me show you the way through.

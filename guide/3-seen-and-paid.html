3The Two Things Your Body Won’t Let You Do

Your partner offers to cover things for a few months so you can build. You say no.

Someone offers to help you, real help, the kind you have actually been praying for. And you find you can’t take it.

A client asks your rate and you hear yourself round the number down before it even leaves your mouth. Or you do the whole thing for free again and tell yourself it’s good for the portfolio.

Here is what I want you to see, because once you see it you cannot unsee it.

Being seen and being paid are the same program, running in two different places in your body.

You already know visibility feels dangerous. What nobody tells you is that receiving feels exactly as dangerous, for exactly the same reason. Asking for more, charging your worth, letting help come toward you, taking up that much space. To a body that learned safety meant staying small, all of that registers as a threat.

So you shrink the number. You carry shame around money even in seasons when you are genuinely broke and the help is sitting right there in front of you.

One woman said it so plainly it stopped me. “My habits toward myself cannot be mirrored in my business.” She treats herself like the intern who hasn’t earned it yet. And then she wonders why the business keeps treating her the same way.

This is the receiving block, and it is not a character flaw. Undercharging was never humility. It was your body doing the only thing it knew how to do to keep you safe.

The body that cannot be seen and the body that cannot be paid are running the same code.

Which is actually good news, Sis. Because it means you do not have two giant problems to solve. You have one. And it is not a problem with your worth or your discipline. It is a nervous system that has been holding its breath for a very long time, waiting for it to be safe to exhale.

No amount of pricing strategy fixes this. No money mindset course fixes this, not on its own, because it talks to the part of you that already agrees with it while the part actually running the show never gets addressed.

The body has to feel safe first. Safe to be seen. Safe to receive. That is the whole game, and almost nobody is playing it at the right level.

So before we talk about how you get free, there’s something you need to see about where you actually are right now.

Because you are not nowhere. You are somewhere very specific on a path. And nobody ever gave you the map.

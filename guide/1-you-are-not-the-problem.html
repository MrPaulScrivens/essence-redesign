1You Are Not the Problem

You opened the app to post something. You typed it out. Then you read it back, found the reason it wasn’t ready yet, and closed the app.

Maybe the lighting was wrong. Maybe it came out wishy-washy, nothing like the way it sounds in your head. Maybe you just didn’t feel like yourself that day, and you told yourself you’d do it tomorrow.

That was a year ago. And a lot of tomorrows have come and gone since then.

Let’s look at the year from the inside, the way only you have seen it.

Overthinking every post before you let yourself hit send. Second-guessing the offer, the prices, the voice, every piece of it. Starting things and stalling out right before they could gain any traction. Watching another woman launch the thing you’ve been sitting on, and feeling that quiet sting you’d never say out loud.

You’ve done all the “right” things and still haven’t signed the clients.

And underneath all of it, the same voice on a loop.

“Who do you think you are.”

“You’re not ready.”

“No one is going to pay you for this.”

“You need one more certification before you can really start.”

You already know those voices aren’t telling the truth. You’ve done the mindset work. You’ve said the affirmations into the mirror on the good mornings. They keep winning anyway.

So you keep collecting evidence against yourself. There’s a TikTok you started and abandoned. A logo you designed and never put anywhere. An offer you wrote out fully and never let anybody buy. A niche you’ve refreshed so many times you’ve lost count.

Every dropped ball makes it a little harder to trust yourself the next time you pick one up.

Here’s the part I need you to actually hear.

You are not lazy, Sis. You have overcome more than most people will ever know about.

You show up for your family and for the whole community that keeps leaning on you. You walk into a room and people feel you before you say a word.

You are not confused about what you want either. You know exactly what you’re called to build. That’s what makes the sitting still so painful.

Don’t get me wrong, the work matters. The offer, the content, the LLC, the outreach, all of it is real and all of it has to get built. But none of that is the reason you froze.

You’re not standing here because you didn’t try hard enough.

You’re standing here because nobody ever told you what was actually holding you in place.

So let’s talk about it.

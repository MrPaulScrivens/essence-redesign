You Are Not the Problem

You opened the app to post something. Typed it out. Read it back, found another reason it wasn’t ready yet, and closed the app.

Maybe the lighting was off. Maybe it came out flat and sounded unnatural. Maybe you just didn’t feel like yourself that day, and you told yourself you’d come back to it tomorrow.

But that was a year ago. And a lot of tomorrows have passed since then.

Let’s look at that year from the inside. The way only you have lived it.

Overthinking every post before you let yourself hit send. Second-guessing your offer, the price, the words, all of it. Starting something and stalling out right before it could get any traction. Watching another woman launch the exact thing you’ve been sitting on, and feeling that quiet sting you wouldn’t dare say out loud.

You’ve done everything you were supposed to do. And the clients still aren’t coming.

And underneath all of it, the same voice running on a loop.

“Who do you think you are?”
“You’re not ready yet.”
“Nobody is going to pay you for this.”
“One more certification and then you can really start.”

You already know those voices are lying. You’ve done the mindset work. You’ve said the affirmations on the good mornings, looked yourself in the mirror and meant it. And they keep winning anyway.

So the evidence against yourself keeps stacking. The account you started and went quiet on. The logo you designed and never used. The offer you wrote out in full and never let anyone buy. The bio you’ve reworked so many times you’ve lost track of where you started.

Every time you drop the ball, it gets a little harder to trust yourself when you pick the next one up.

Here’s what I need you to actually hear.

You are not lazy. You have carried things most people will never know about. You show up for your family, for the people in your community who need you, for everyone who leans on you first. You walk into a room and people feel you before you ever open your mouth.

You are not confused about what you want. You know exactly what you’re called to build. That’s the part that makes the staying still so painful.

The work matters — I won’t pretend it doesn’t. The offer, the content, the LLC, the outreach, all of it is real and all of it gets built. But none of that is why you froze.

You’re not standing here because you didn’t want it badly enough.

You’re standing here because nobody ever told you what was actually keeping you in place.

So let’s get into it.

It’s Not a Discipline Problem. It’s a Safety Problem.

You think the reason you’re not moving is that you don’t want it badly enough.

You have wanted this so badly, for so long, that the wanting has started to feel like grief.

So let me tell you what is actually happening, because it is not what you’ve been told.

The gap between knowing what to do and actually doing it is not a gap in your discipline. Your body learned, a long time ago, that being seen was not safe. That taking up space invited something you didn’t want. So every time you get close to being visible, close to declaring yourself, close to charging what you’re worth, close to letting people really look — your nervous system does the job it was built to do. It pulls you back.

The spiral. The sudden need to reorganize your entire offer before you can post a single thing. That is not procrastination. That is protection. Your body is keeping you safe from a threat it still believes is real.

This is why everything you’ve already tried didn’t stick.

Mindset programs worked on your mind while your body kept quietly running the show. Strategy programs handed you a playbook your body would not let you run. Accountability programs told you what to do and never once asked why you kept stopping. Masculine hustle programs piled more pressure onto a nervous system that was already past full — and you burned out and ended up further from your vision than when you started.

I’ve been there. I invested in coaches, did the tasks, got things done — and still felt crunchy in my body the whole time. Dreading the calls. Anxiety through the roof. Building things that looked right on the outside while my nervous system was screaming on the inside. None of it was built for a woman whose body reads visibility as danger. None of it reached the place the block actually lives.

Willpower is real and you have plenty of it. You have used it to get through things that would have broken other people. But willpower is a force you push down onto your body, and a body in survival mode pushes right back. You cannot force your way into feeling safe. It doesn’t work like that for anyone.

And there’s a deeper layer here, especially for us as women of color.

Perfectionism taught you that you had to earn your worth before you were allowed to move. Be twice as good. Look a certain way before it’s okay to be seen. That is survival masking as excellence, and so much of it traces directly back to what this world asks of women of color. Of course your body is tired. You have been the firstborn daughter holding everyone together for twenty+ years. Of course something deep in you says not yet every time you try to put yourself first.

You are not failing at this.

Your body is doing exactly what it learned to do to keep you safe. Once you really understand that, everything about the way you’ve been stuck starts to make sense.

There’s one more layer underneath this one. And it’s the part almost nobody talks about.

The Two Things Your Body Won’t Let You Do

Your partner offers to cover things for a few months so you can build. You say no.

Someone offers to help you — real help, the kind you have genuinely been praying for. And when it arrives, you find you can’t receive it.

A client asks your rate and you hear yourself round the number down before it even leaves your mouth. Or you do the whole thing for free again and tell yourself it’s good for the portfolio.

Here is what I want you to see. Because once you see it, you really cannot unsee it.

Being seen and being paid are the same program, running in two different places in your body.

You already know visibility feels dangerous. What nobody tells you is that receiving feels just as dangerous, for the exact same reason. Asking for more, charging your worth, letting support come toward you, taking up that much space — to a body that learned safety meant staying small, all of that registers as a threat.

So you shrink the number. You carry shame around money even in the seasons when you are genuinely stretched and the help is sitting right there in front of you.

I had a client say something to me once that I still think about. “My habits toward myself cannot be mirrored in my business.” She had been treating herself like the intern who hadn’t earned it yet. And then wondering why the business kept reflecting that same energy back.

This is the receiving block. And it is not a character flaw, boo. Undercharging was never humility. It was your body doing the only thing it knew how to do to keep you safe.

The body that cannot be seen and the body that cannot be paid are running the same code.

And that is actually good news. Because it means you do not have two giant problems. You have one. It is not a problem with your worth or your discipline. It is a nervous system that has been holding its breath for a long time, waiting for the moment it finally feels safe enough to exhale.

No pricing strategy fixes this on its own. No money mindset course reaches it alone — because it speaks to the part of you that already agrees, while the part actually running the show never gets touched.

The body has to feel safe first. Safe to be seen. Safe to receive. That is the whole game. And most people are trying to play it from the neck up.

So before we talk about how you get free, there’s something you need to see about where you are right now.

Because you are not nowhere. You are somewhere very specific on a path. And nobody ever gave you the map.

Where You Actually Are

There is a reason the confusion has felt so personal. You think the stopping and restarting, the not having it figured out yet, means something is specifically wrong with you.

It doesn’t. You are on a path that has stages. And almost nobody ever gets shown the path.

This is the Journey of a Soul-Led Entrepreneur. Six stages. Read through slowly and notice where you feel yourself standing.

Stage One. The Calling.

It arrives like a knowing you didn’t choose. A purpose, an offering, placed in you by God/Spirit/Source, that simply will not leave you alone. This is where you finally say yes to it. For many of us, it is the first time we have ever really chosen ourselves.

For me, that yes came in 2020 — quiet at first, then impossible to ignore. I ignored it anyway and went back to a 9-5. The calling waited but got louder and louder.

Stage Two. The Clarity. (you might be here)

This stage humbles you. Clarity doesn’t arrive while you’re sitting still thinking about it. It arrives through action — through the audacity to try, one session and one post at a time. You gather breadcrumbs that slowly begin to reveal who you are here for, what you are really here to do, and what lights you up about this work.

I finally surrendered to the calling and launched my business, Essence of Freedom, in 2024 and spent this stage testing, launching, trying things that weren’t quite right — including work that paid well but felt like a betrayal of my body every single time. Clarity only came from moving, not from thinking.

Stage Three. The Foundation. (or you might be here)

You’ve found the thread. Now you build. The brand, the voice, the offer — the real bones of the thing — all while your visibility anxiety is screaming in the background. This is exactly where the nervous system work stops being optional. Because the block was never in the strategy. It lives in the body.

The moment I found my language — helping women of color move from fear into confident action — people responded immediately. The foundation came together fast once the clarity arrived.

Stage Four. The Authority.

You show up so consistently that something shifts in you that no strategy could have manufactured. Your gifts stop feeling like questions and start feeling like certainties. The money hasn’t caught up to the energy yet, and this is the messy middle — but people begin to see you as the expert you have always been.

I posted on social media every single day. I watched my offerings expand into something sacred and unrecognizable from where it started and people were responding. Something in me rewrote itself. The devotion does that.

Stage Five. The Flow.

The compound interest of every stage before this one finally pays out. People seek you out. They arrive already ready — because you built something real and kept showing up long enough to be found.

This is where I am now. Women who have never met me in person know my name and seek me out specifically. The foundation is holding weight the way it was always meant to.

Stage Six. The Expansion.

Everything you survived and everything you became turns into something bigger than you. The business becomes a vehicle for more than income. It becomes legacy. One woman rising and reaching back for the next.

Sit with where you landed.

If you recognized yourself in Stage Two or Stage Three, you are exactly who this guide was written for. The Clarity and the Foundation are the two stages where almost everyone gets stuck. And they are the two stages where the body work becomes everything — because this is where you are being asked to show up before your nervous system believes it is safe to do so.

Here is what I need you to hold onto.

Nearly half of new businesses don’t make it. Not because those women weren’t built for it. Because nobody gave them the map. Nobody told them the confusion was a stage and not a verdict. Nobody told them the inconsistency was never proof they should quit.

You are standing in the exact place the journey gets hard for everyone. Holding your breath. Wondering if the fact that it’s hard means it isn’t really yours.

It’s yours.

Now let me show you the way through.
